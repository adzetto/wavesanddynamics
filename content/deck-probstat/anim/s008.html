Distribution models: the shape follows the mechanism

A distribution is the simplest probabilistic model: it spreads probability over the possible values of one quantity. How the quantity is produced suggests the family.

Normal

sum of many small effects

measurement error, dead load

Lognormal

positive; product of factors

strength, stiffness, fatigue life

Gumbel (extreme value)

largest value per period

annual peak flood, wind

Weibull

weakest link fails first

brittle strength, component life

Exponential

waiting time, no memory

time to next vehicle or event

Poisson

count of rare events

trucks per hour, quakes per year

Binomial

successes in 𝑛 trials

defective bolts in a sample of 10

Beta

a fraction between 0 and 1

percent damaged, a probability

Ask how the value is produced; the mechanism suggests the distribution family.
