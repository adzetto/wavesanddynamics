𝜇 = unknown population mean strength

SE = 𝜎/√𝑛 = 3/√25 = 0.6 MPa

𝑍 = (𝑋̄ − 𝜇)/SE ∼ 𝑁(0, 1)

Each tail contains 2.5%.

1.96 is the 97.5th percentile of 𝑁(0, 1).

Here 𝑁(mean, variance) denotes a normal distribution.
