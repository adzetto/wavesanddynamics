Bayesian strength example: the actual calculation

Same strength data: 𝑛 = 25, 𝑥̄ = 32 MPa, known 𝜎 = 3 MPa. Prior mean 30 MPa and prior SD 2 MPa.

Prior variance 𝑣0 = 22 = 4 MPa2

Data variance 𝑣𝐷 = 32/25 = 0.36 MPa2

𝑣𝑝 = 1/(1/4 + 1/0.36) = 0.3303 MPa2

Posterior SD = √𝑣𝑝 = 0.575 MPa

𝑚𝑝 = (30/4 + 32/0.36)/(1/4 + 1/0.36)

𝑚𝑝 = 0.0826 × 30 + 0.9174 × 32

𝑚𝑝 = 31.83 MPa

About 8% prior weight and 92% data weight.
