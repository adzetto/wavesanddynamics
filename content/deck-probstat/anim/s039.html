Model outputs and decision rules are different

A single number can still contain probabilistic information. A decision needs a rule for using that information.

Model outputWhat it tells youPossible decision rule
Point prediction 𝑦̂A predicted response, often a meanCompare with an operating limit
Quantile 𝑞0.05 or 𝑞0.99A specified model tail levelUse a chosen design percentile
Exceedance probabilityChance an outcome crosses a limitRequire probability below a target
Reliability index 𝛽A scalar reliability summaryCompare with a consistent 𝛽 target

Comparing 𝛽 with a target is a reliability-based decision, even though 𝛽 is a scalar.
