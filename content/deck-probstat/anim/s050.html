What correlation and spectra help us do

The same calculations answer different physical questions, provided the signal and model assumptions fit.

Structural frequencies

PSD peaks can suggest modal frequencies. Separate structural response from forcing peaks.

Damping

Model the decay of modal correlations under suitable linear-system and excitation assumptions.

Speech and music

Repeated autocorrelation peaks suggest a period. Pitch is approximately its reciprocal.

Radar and sonar

Cross-correlate an echo with the transmitted pulse. Delay gives travel time and range.

EEG rhythms

Band power summarizes rhythmic content. Artifacts can create misleading peaks.

Financial returns

Lag correlations reveal dependence to test further, not a guaranteed trading rule.

A spectral peak is evidence to interpret, not an automatic diagnosis.
