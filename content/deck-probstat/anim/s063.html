Estimation theory across disciplines

The common task is to infer an unknown quantity from imperfect observations. The field changes the target and the data.

FieldUnknown targetObserved dataShared methods
StatisticsPopulation mean, variance or regression coefficientsA sample of strength testsMoments, MLE, Bayesian estimates
Signal processingHidden displacement, velocity or a clean signalNoisy sensor measurements over timeLeast squares, MMSE, Kalman filtering
Machine learningPredictive model weights or a latent representationTraining inputs and observed outcomesLikelihood, regularized loss, MAP
Data scienceParameters and predictions used in an analysisCollected, cleaned and combined datasetsRegression, MLE, Bayesian estimation
OptimizationThe parameter value that minimizes an objectiveA loss function, constraints and possibly dataNumerical solver for LS, MLE or MAP

Same calculation, different language

Gaussian noise: least squares = MLE.  Add a Gaussian prior: quadratic regularization = MAP.Assumptions: independent Gaussian errors of equal, fixed variance.

Optimization computes many estimates; the statistical model and loss explain what those estimates mean.
