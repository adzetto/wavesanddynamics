Estimation: what exactly is unknown?

The target and the available data determine whether we fit a model, filter a state, smooth a history or predict a future value.

TargetExampleTypical output
ParameterMaterial mean, regression slope, stiffnessPoint estimate and uncertainty
Current hidden stateBridge displacement from noisy sensorsFiltered state estimate
Past hidden stateReconstruct yesterday’s response using later dataSmoothed history
Future responseTraffic demand or displacement at the next stepPredictive distribution

“Batch versus recursive” describes computation. “Parameter versus state” describes the target.
