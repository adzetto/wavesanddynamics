One Kalman update, step by step

Zoom into one hour of the previous slide. The model predicts 10 mm (SD 2 mm); the sensor reads 14 mm (noise SD 3 mm). Where is the displacement?

The two sources

Prior = model prediction: 10 mm, SD 2 mmSensor reading: 14 mm, SD 3 mm

Combine them

Gain 𝐾 = 22 / (22 + 32) = 4/13 = 0.31

Estimate = 10 + 0.31 × 4 = 11.23 mm

New SD = √((1 − 𝐾) × 4) = 1.66 mm

Why not just trust the sensor?

Its error (SD 3) is larger than the model's (SD 2), so the model gets more weight.
