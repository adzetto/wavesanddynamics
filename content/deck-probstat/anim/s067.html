One idea behind estimation: weight by precision

Two different examples from earlier in this guide. They look unrelated, but both combine an old estimate with new data using exactly the same rule.

Case A: concrete strength (slides 18–19)

2426283032343638MPaprior belief: 30 MPa (SD 2)25 tests: 32 MPa (SE 0.6)combined: 31.83 MPa

Old 30 MPa (variance 4), data 32 MPa (variance 0.36). 𝑤 = 4 / 4.36 = 0.92 → 31.83 MPa

Case B: bridge displacement (slides 65–66)

510152025mmmodel prediction:
10 mm (SD 2)sensor: 14 mm (SD 3)combined: 11.23 mm

Old 10 mm (variance 4), data 14 mm (variance 9). 𝑤 = 4 / 13 = 0.31 → 11.23 mm

Same rule: new = old + 𝑤 × (data − old), 𝑤 = var(old) / [var(old) + var(data)]

Different problems, same rule: each source counts in proportion to its precision.
