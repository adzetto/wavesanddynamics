# About Me (`parts/about.py`), round 10

- **Interface** `render_page(about_html, links=None, areas=None, timeline_html=None) -> str` returns the whole page body, `<div class="wrap abt">` included, with the page's `<h1>` (build.py's `wrap_page()` passes it through untouched). `about_html` is `PROF_ABOUT`; `links` and `areas` are `cv.json`'s (`links`: dicts with `label`, `href`, `kind`); `timeline_html` is `parts/timeline.py`'s Career section, rendered here when it is None or "". `None` for links or areas means his CV's own values (fallbacks kept in the module); an empty list means none, and the actions row or the areas column is then left out.
- **Order** H1 "About Me"; the profile (portrait at 120px, the h2 "Korkut Kaynardag, PhD" (a heading in the markup as it is on the page), his post); the actions row; then "Research areas" and "Biography"; then Career; then At a glance. In the markup the areas come before the biography, so wherever the page is one column they follow the profile directly and are seen as the page opens.
- **Layout** The columns are a size container (`.abt__cols`). From 800px of content width: biography left (up to `--measure`), areas right, as wide as the longest area (288px at 16px serif, so none wraps); at 1280x800 the whole list and its link end at y 684. The areas stay beside the biography while it scrolls (sticky, `top:40px`) where the column is on screen (`width > 1000px`) and the window is at least 680px tall. From 560 to 799px: the areas in two columns under the actions. Under 560px: one list, then the biography.
- **Actions** Download CV to `cv.html` is the site's one filled button (`--accent`, white 5.55:1; hover `--accent-hover`, press `--accent-press` and `scale(.97)`), with a line glyph of his deck's CV icon. Google Scholar, LinkedIn, ResearchGate follow as underlined sans links with a leaning arrow; GitHub and YouTube a step quieter (`--muted`, 400). Links are http(s) only, `rel="me"`, and this site's own address (his CV's "Personal webpage") is dropped. Every target is 44px tall.
- **His words** `about_html` goes in as given. `embolden()` puts `<strong>` round the eight runs he set in bold on slide 2 (`BOLD`) only where they are not bold already, and adds tags only; it is a no-op on build.py's current `PROF_ABOUT`, which carries the same `<strong>` runs, so the character-for-character check holds. Areas are escaped, nothing more. No em dash anywhere.
- **Research areas: the beaded chain** Serif 16px in `--ink`: his words, which never move. Beside each a bead (`<span class="abt__bead" aria-hidden="true">`, a 9px ring with the paper inside) on one dotted thread, like the home drawing's sensors, in `--link` 45% into the paper (the Career route's blue). *The signal*: each time the chain comes into view (as the page opens, and again whenever it has left the screen and come back; the script's observer adds `.is-run` when its top is on screen with 30% of it, or 90% of it is, and takes it away when the chain is off screen) a light runs down it after a 240ms breath at 60ms a bead: the thread lights from bead to bead (linear, a signal's even pace), each bead fills as the light reaches it, swells to 1.25 and settles (520ms), the lit thread goes out two beads behind, and the first and last bead each send out one ring. Three or four beads are lit at once; the run is over in about 2s. *The pointer* (mouse or trackpad only, `(hover:hover) and (pointer:fine)`): the bead beside it fills in `--link` and swells to 1.33 (240ms), the thread draws out to the beads on either side (240ms, from the bead outward) and they deepen and swell to 1.15 as it arrives (120ms later); one faint ring leaves the bead after 60ms. Leaving takes 120ms and the thread goes back toward the bead it came from. Moving down the list moves that lit window along the chain; transitions retarget, so it never restarts. From 560 to 799px of content the areas stand in two columns with no thread: the signal runs bead by bead and the pointer's bead answers alone. Everything is laid over the chain at rest and returns to it: nothing is hidden before it, nothing waits for it, no layout moves (transform and opacity only).
- **At a glance** Below Career. Each figure links to its CV section: `cv.html#journal-publications`, `#patents`, `#awards` (cv.json's section ids; build.py's link report fails `--strict` if one stops resolving). Card vocabulary: hairline top; under the pointer the 4% ink fill unrolls down from the hairline (`scaleY` on `--spring-mid`; out in 120ms, rolled back up only once gone), label underline, the site's arrow drawn; a key gets that end state at once; a press deepens the fill to 7% and the card gives to .98 on `--spring-quick`. The reveal (round 10), once, if the figures were below the fold when the page opened: the hairline draws, the figure rises out of its line box on `--spring-slow` and settles, a 2px blur clearing as it slows, the words follow; 70ms between cards. The figure and the label are spans set as blocks, not paragraphs: inside a link they are one phrase, and a large `<p>` over a smaller one reads as a heading to axe's p-as-heading.
- **States and access** Hover only under `(hover:hover)` (the chain under `(pointer:fine)` too), focus rings `var(--focus)`. Keyboard focus gets the hover's end state at once, with no travel (the profile links' leaning arrow, My Research Areas and the glance arrows, as parts/hero.py does; round-4 finding RM-7). Reduced motion: no signal, no rings, no reveal, arrows finished, no press scale, the glance fill appears in place; the pointer's bead and links change in place. Forced colours: the button keeps an edge, thread and beads take `CanvasText`, the lights go and the pointer's bead takes `Highlight`. Print: no lights. Checked: axe 0 violations at 1280 and 390, and with the experimental and best-practice rules nothing of this part's (the one hit left is the column's `.id` link, build.py's), keyboard focus instant, reduced motion and forced colours in Chromium, the signal and the pointer frame by frame (build/r5/about-shots).
- **Scope** Selectors under `.abt` and `.glance`, keyframes `abt-`/`glance-`, tokens read only. JS: an empty touch listener (iOS `:active`), the chain's observer (it stays attached so the signal can run again; the About page has no endless animation, so it costs no frames), the glance reveal.
