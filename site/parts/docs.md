# docs: one column, and a contents card that leaves no trace when shut

One column, `--measure-doc` (672px, theme.py), for text, figures, tables, callouts, captions and the title, centred in `.main` by a three-track grid (`minmax(0,1fr) minmax(0,672px) minmax(0,1fr)`); the footer takes the same width (`.foot--doc`). Folding the site's column moves the text, never rewraps it.
Figures (preview.py `_image`, sizes from build.py `figure_meta`): never wider than the column or their pixels; a picture the author set narrower in Word keeps that share (`fig--inset`, `--inset`, floored at 60%, all phones full width); `srcset` 720w/1344w; `fig--zoom` links to the full file, with a mark under the corner on touch screens. Pictures in tables and flows are bare, at half their pixels; a sentence's equation is `img.eq`, 1.12em tall, in the line.
Tables fill the column and scroll inside it with edge shadows; five columns and up are 14px (`tbl--many`), seven 13px (`tbl--dense`); `.c` centres a column of short values; `<colgroup>` carries Word's widths for up to four columns.
Contents in the text (`#contents`, `<details open>`, markup from build.py `page_doc`, unchanged): a card in `--card`, no border or shadow (a transparent one shows under forced colours). The summary row is the toggle: the list mark (a CSS mask, `--toc-ico`), his label in Source Sans 3 600 17px ink, "Hide"/"Show" (drawn, `content: ... / ""`, so screen readers hear only expanded/collapsed) and a chevron pointing where the list will go; shut, "· N sections" joins the label (not under 481px) and the card is that row alone. Rows are 48px at least, numbered in a right-aligned gutter the titles hang clear of: his `.hn` where his headings carry numbers (ML guide), else a CSS counter hidden from screen readers. Hairlines start at the titles; a lit row takes `--ink` 5% over the card and its muted number steps to `--body` (muted is 4.20:1 on the lit card). The card keeps the last chapter read marked (`.is-last`: 3px `--accent` rail, title in ink) for a reader who comes back up to it.
The dock (`.tocdock`, script only): the Contents button is the card made small (`--card` pill, the same mark), and the list it opens is numbered the same way on a hairline track with one warm mark (`.toc__mark`) for the section being read, repositioned by a ResizeObserver whenever the list reflows. In the margin (1424px up, or 1240px up with the site's column folded) the list sits beside the text, default open, the choice kept in localStorage `wad:toc` and restored in `<head>` (`data-toc`). Narrower desktops: the list opens over the text from the left, with a scrim (`data-tocpanel`, never stored); under 1188px the button is its mark with a tooltip. Outside the margin the button shows only while the card in the text is off screen (not yet reached, or passed), so the page never offers two Contents at once. 1000px and down: the pill floats in from the top of a document whose card is still below the screen (the ML guide's is 4.4 screens down on a phone) and, once the card is passed, while the reader scrolls back up; it opens the list as a bottom sheet. Rows 38px for a pointer, 46px for touch; the panel's head label sits on the titles' edge.
Motion: in 240ms `--ease`, out in 160ms; transitions, so interruptible; the card's fold changes its height at once (the long document under it is laid out once, not every frame: 2 layouts a fold on the ML guide, was 16) and turns the chevron; opened by the reader, its rows fade in and settle 4px (200ms, the script's, never at load); a pressed button gives to .97; none of it under reduced motion. Focus goes to the current section on open and back to the button on close; Escape, the scrim, the x and choosing a section close an overlay.
Script: 60 lines (over the contract's 40, for the three modes, the focus rules and the card's mark). No script: the card only, with its toggle. Print: the card open, unfilled, with a hairline edge; no dock, no toggle words.
