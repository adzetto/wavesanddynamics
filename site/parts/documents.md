# documents: the Big Picture of Waves and Data Analytics (big-picture.html)

`render_big_picture(docs)` returns the whole page body (`<div class="wrap bp">` with its own `<h1>`, which `wrap_page()` keeps). `docs` is build.DOCS (slug -> (title, note, ...)); without it a local copy of the seven titles and notes is used.

`CATEGORIES` (ROUND5_SPEC 1, exactly its table) is the one list of the Big Picture's topics: `dict(id, label, docs=[slugs], soon=href or None)`, in order waves, dynamics, signal-processing, system-identification, estimation-theory, optimization, machine-learning, probability-statistics, python-programming, communication. The column's Big Picture list is built from it: each item opens `big-picture.html#<id>`. Labels are plain text ("Probability & Statistics" has an ampersand): escape them.

The page is an index. `families()` groups the topics: a topic that shares a document with the one before it joins its group, so a document shows once for all the topics it covers (Waves and Dynamics over the vibrations and waves guide; Signal Processing, System Identification, Estimation Theory and Optimization over the signal processing guide); a topic in preparation stands alone. The groups are rows 5 to 10 of his column, and carry no names of ours. Each group is an `<h2>` of its topic names (one per line, `id` = the topic's id, a hidden comma between names for screen readers) beside its rows. A row is one link to a whole document (title, the note from build.DOCS, the site's arrow) or to the topic's page in preparation (his sub-line, "In preparation", named first by the page's own title for a screen reader). A document that covers only some of its group's topics names them in a small label (From Bridges to Photons: "Waves"). No link targets a section of a document. "From my research" (eyebrow) closes the page with the three SHM/NDT documents.

Layout: the index is a query container. From 680px of its own width the topics take a column (min 232px, so every name keeps to one line) and the documents stand beside them, baselines aligned; below that the documents hang under their topics from a hairline, a step smaller.

Arriving (`:target`, no script needed): the landing puts the group's top edge 28px under the window (about 24px under the phone's bar) via `scroll-margin-top` by the topic's place in its group (`--k`), so its documents are on screen with it. The topic's rail (3px `--accent`, the column's "you are here" mark) draws in from its middle (240ms, 100ms delay) and stays while the address points there; each document that covers the topic takes one `--wash` that sweeps across the row from the topic's side (360ms), holds and fades (1.5s in all, 80ms between rows). The wash rules are generated from CATEGORIES (`:has(#id:target) [data-for~=id]`). Transform, clip-path and opacity on pseudo-elements only: no layout shift (CLS 0 measured on every arrival). Reduced motion: the rail stands, nothing plays. Forced colours: the rail in Highlight, no wash. The script (10 lines) only replays the moment when the topic already in the address is pressed again (no :target change).

States: row fill 5% ink (8% on press), arrow draws on hover; keyboard focus gets the ring and the arrow's end state at once (RM-7).

Checked (build/r5/bigpicture-src/check.py, headless Chromium): axe 0 at 1280 and 390, both motion settings, at rest and mid-wash; no `doc/*.html#` link; every arrival lands with its documents in view; Lighthouse desktop and mobile a11y/BP/SEO 100 (the one experimental failure is the shell's identity link).
