# hero: the home page hero, laid out as his slide 1
`render(contact=None, intro=None, bio=None, art=None)`. `intro` and `bio` are his HTML (PROF_INTRO, PROF_BIO), inserted as given; a None falls back to an exact copy. `art` is parts/heroart.py's figure: it goes into `.hero2__art` beside his list (a figure there keeps no margin; the art sizes itself to the box). `contact` is accepted, so build.py's call stands, and not drawn: since round 5 the home page has no portrait and no address. His photo heads the column on every page (build.py's shell); the column and the footer give his department, institution and city, the Contact page his address.
Layout follows `.hero2`'s own width (container queries). From 860px: his name, his numbered list and the two controls beside the illustration, in two equal columns (443px each at a 924px column: his 399px lede keeps one line and his 698px sub-line two, with 10% to spare, so the Georgia fallback sets them alike). The drawing keeps the top of its column, level with the name's cap line; his column runs on under it with the controls. Then, 47px down, "Myself:" and his paragraph at `--measure`. Under 860px: one column, name, list, controls, illustration (at most 640px), "Myself:". The markup order is that order at every width, so focus and a screen reader meet his words and the two ways onward before the drawing's keys.
"Myself:" is set as the page's h2 (serif 600 28/1.22, 21px over the paragraph), because his slide sets it exactly as it sets "Motivation for Creating the Educational Sections": the biography reads as the page's first section, not as a caption hanging from the hero. render() adds `.hero2__bio--head` when the biography opens with a paragraph that is one bold run (his `<p><strong>Myself:</strong></p>`), and only that paragraph is restyled; his markup is untouched. An `<h2>` in its place (ROUND4 finding A11Y-3 asks build.py's PROF_BIO for `<h2 class="hero2__bioh">Myself:</h2>`) takes the same rule, so the page does not move when that lands.
The name opens the hero with nothing above it; a -.25em top margin puts its cap line on the top of the illustration (Source Serif 4 sets the cap line .25em into the line box). It is not `text-box` trimming, which trims to whichever font is on screen: the Georgia fallback made the name 3.1px shorter and the whole hero shifted on the font swap.
Controls, 32px under his list and 12px apart; they open the list's first two items one step deeper. About me is the brief's ghost button: 1px `--line-strong` (3.49:1), ink label; hover fills `--card` and darkens the outline to ink (160ms); press takes `--rule` at once. My research areas is a `--link` label (6.31:1 on `--card`) with its two-path arrow in a 28px `--card` disc. On hover a `--card` capsule under the whole control fades in (160ms) and grows from 94% about the disc's centre (240ms `--ease`), so the disc seems to open into it, while the arrow reaches 3px (240ms); letting go takes 120ms. Only transform, opacity and stroke-dashoffset move: round 4's clip-path sweep is gone (finding RM-6). Press: `--rule` at once (label 5.56:1) and `scale(.97)`. Keyboard focus: the end state at once, no travel, plus the 2px `--focus` ring. Reduced motion: the capsule simply appears; no growth, no arrow travel, no press scale. Forced colours: a LinkText border.
Font swap: nothing in the hero changes size when Source Serif 4 replaces the Georgia fallback (the name has a fixed line box; the label's line box comes from its own 600 face in both fonts; bold runs in the paragraph take `line-height:1`). Measured on cold loads in headless Chromium: CLS 0 at 1280x800, 1350x940, 1440x900 and 390x844, with and without reduced motion.
JS: one empty passive `touchstart` on `.hero2`, so iOS shows `:active`. Tokens are read, never written; no custom property is set here (round 4's `--hero2-tint*` are gone, finding F5).
For theme.py's owner: `[data-side="closed"] .hero2__id{display:revert}` no longer has a target, since the home page has no address card.
