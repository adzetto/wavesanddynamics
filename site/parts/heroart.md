# heroart: the home page illustration
`render() -> str` returns the whole `<figure class="ha">`: a `.ha__stage` holding one inline SVG (`role="img"`, one label), the layers that move over it, the toolbar of thirteen keys, then the line `.ha__read` under the drawing. `CSS` and `JS` are module strings like every part; the script runs once per page (it guards itself). IDs are prefixed `ha-`; render the figure once per page. The figure is a size container (`container: ha / inline-size`), so give it a definite width: parts/hero.py's `minmax(0,1fr)` track does. Markup 57.8 KB (limit 60), CSS 25 KB, JS 5.1 KB; 67 CSS animations and 12 SMIL animations while it plays.

**The story.** The reference JPEG redrawn in its own pixel space (crop `20 66 1260 812`), a 12 s loop that tells cause and effect three times, left to right, and starts and ends on the reference frame (also the reduced-motion, no-script and print frame). A: the acoustic source at the tall building's foot rings, the building sways, its sensor fires, a packet climbs to the regression, which refits. B: the train leaves and the next arrives, the bridge's main span swells under them, the tower sensor fires, the network fires layer by layer. C: the car drives off at the picture's edge and comes back to park, its hum is heard, the message hops car, chimney, airplane and reaches the tree, whose leaf turns amber. Around it the rotor turns, smoke rises, the airplane glides, the trace scrolls into the ripple and the bars breathe.

**Round 10 (27 Sep 2026).** The professor saw the airplane lacked a wing underneath: seen from the ground it shows its planform, so it now has both tailplanes (the near one the far one mirrored, in the near wing's paint, the tail cone rounded alike). Every vibration is a mode as the structure has it: the wings bend up and down together (the symmetric first bending mode, the cut on a cantilever's first mode, each tip moving 4.6 to 8.5 units up or down the picture, as the world's vertical projects from below) where they used to turn fore and aft in their own plane; the tall building's upper half sways in step with the whole (the roof used to lead the middle by 0.2 s, which no mode of a building fixed at its foot does); the span keeps its first symmetric mode. The car is the same both ways about its middle, so it drives forward out of the picture and forward back in (it used to reverse out), and it settles as it stops. The layers' SVGs take their size from the stylesheet (1.7 KB less markup).

**Interaction.** Thirteen real `<button>`s over the drawing, one `role="toolbar"` ("Trace the data") with a roving tab stop: arrows, Home, End; Enter or Space pins (`aria-pressed`), Escape or a press elsewhere releases. A lit key keeps its structure, sensors, links and model and steps the rest to 34%; the line under the drawing names the pair (`aria-hidden`, its height always kept). On a mouse the far hills drift against the pointer on a critically damped spring; nothing linked moves.

**Stillness.** It always plays (no pause button, the professor's rule): it pauses only off screen and in a hidden tab (`.ha:not([data-run]) *`, looked at on scroll and resize, no IntersectionObserver), and nothing moves until it is first seen. Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` it is the static reference frame and the keys still light paths. Print shows the static frame without the controls.

**Narrow.** `@container ha (max-width: 400px)`: the dotted links go, the dashed take 1px, rings and packets grow by radius, the trace gains weight. Every key keeps a 24px floor.

**Checked.** tests/test_heroart.py (27 tests: the modes, the loop from rest to rest, the tailplanes, the car's symmetry, the wings' first mode and amplitudes, the markup budget). Frames every 0.1 s through the loop at 1440 (DPR 3 to 5), phone (390, DPR 3), dark theme and WebKit; no console output. Main thread at the top of the page, headless Chromium at 60 Hz: 4 to 5 ms a frame, as before this round, almost all of it the SMIL repaint; 1 ms with the figure scrolled away.

**Known limits.** The rotor's reflection and the bars' faint reflection stand still (`<use>` clones, where Chrome runs no CSS animation), under a fade.
