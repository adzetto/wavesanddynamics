# masthead: the band across the top of the home page
`render(text_html)`: his sentence (build.py's PROF_HEADER, inline HTML) goes in exactly as given, inside `<p class="masthead__text">`; a string that already opens with `<p` is kept whole in a div. The root is a plain `<div class="masthead">`: the landmark is shell()'s `.mastrow`. Every selector is under `.masthead`; no `:root` write, no em dash. Markup 3.1 KB, CSS 5.2 KB, JS 21.6 KB (as shipped, comments stripped).

Type: Source Serif 4, clamp(16px, 1.47cqi, 17.5px)/1.55 in a 1100px box, centred and balanced, three lines on a desktop; `--nav-ink` taken 12% toward the navy so a lit phrase can go full white. His marked phrases are CSS Custom Highlights the script finds in his text: "wave motion" wavy in `--mh-sky`, "discrete measurements" dotted, "Data analytics" in `--mh-rose` (a light crimson, no orange). His power of ten is MathML. Under 640px of band: 16px, left-aligned on the page's 22px edge. Band margins 20px over the first line, 20px under the figure (measured: 23px of navy over the first ink, 21.5px under the last).

The figure (29 Sep 2026; the client then: "very stylish, clear and beautiful") tells his sentence in five stages, each from a model: a cable-stayed bridge (150 + 350 + 150 m) raised by balanced cantilevers, the pylons first, the deck growing out from both at once, each stay let down as the front passes its anchor, the side spans landing, the main span closing at its middle; then the deck swinging in its first mode (finite differences, 0.462 Hz, checked against a closed form and Rayleigh-Ritz) in real time. The closing point is taken out as a lens: an atom, its Bohr orbit and the de Broglie wave five wavelengths round (2 pi r = 5 lambda). The wave leaves the atom and drifts right; samples drop onto it, x[n] = x(nT), and the line fades; each sample's sinc rises and their Whittaker-Shannon sum draws the recovered wave through them in `--mh-rose`. Line art: waves and pylons 1.5px, the recovered wave 1.75px, stays, axis and sincs one device pixel, straight lines snapped to the pixel grid, supports with mitred apexes on the deck's underside; nothing touches the canvas's edges.

Formulas: Computer Modern by site/mathtex.py, 15px (14px in a band under 380px), each over its stage, all on one measured baseline in a row of their own. The row is two pairs about the wave at the centre, the physical world and the data: the samples' stretch is sized so the space inside the right pair equals the left pair's (121px each on a 1100px strip), each formula keeping 12px either side and the wave at least 1.25 wavelengths; a one-row strip too narrow for that shows x[n] and x-hat(t) short. Coloured as the figure: u in the deck's blue, lambda in the wave's, x[n] white as the samples, x-hat crimson as its wave.

Story, 9 s: stages at 0.3, 1.78, 3.25, 4.6, 6.05 s; the deck closes at 1.57 s; all stages come forward again from 7.85 s, left to right, and the figure is still at 9.0 s. One stage is told at a time: a told stage steps back to 38%, one not yet told is not drawn, his phrase for it lights in six steps and hands over to the next (a crossfade, never a gap). A pen point, white with a soft light of its line's colour, rides the head of every stroke being drawn (the deck's four fronts, the orbit, the wave, the recovery). A formula arrives on Motion's spring (bounce 0) out of a 3px blur, and steps back with its stage; strokes draw with manim's smooth. The figure comes to rest as a composed final figure: the deck held sagging at the top of a swing and the orbit's wave at its fullest, each at the moment its speed is zero (8.65 s, 8.63 s), the drift eased to a stop (speed falling as (1 - x)^2 over 1 s). Every frame, formulas and lit phrases included, is a pure function of the clock (`band.__mh.at(t)`); back by the browser's history the figure is whole at once.

A phone (a band under 640px): two rows, 128px, one for each half of his sentence: the bridge, the atom and the wave running off the row's end; then the same wave's samples from the next row's start and their recovery, every formula whole. The wave keeps one phase across the break (everything is measured along the signal from its end).

The pointer is a probe: over the figure it reads the stage under it (a ring on the deck, on the matter wave at the pointer's angle round the nucleus, on the wave, on the nearest sample, on the recovered wave, with a stem from the axis for a height); that stage and its formula come forward, the others step back to 32%, its phrase lights. Over his sentence, a phrase under the pointer brings its stage forward. A tap does both on a touch screen, for 2.2 s. After the story the stage under the pointer lives: the deck swings on (the ring riding it) and the orbit's wave breathes; let go, each runs on to the end of its period, its pose in the still figure, and stops.

Quiet: requestAnimationFrame runs only while the story is told on screen in a visible tab, and while the pointer's focus or a woken stage moves; at rest there is none, and no idle loop (the old drifting idle is gone: the still figure is the ending). A passive scroll listener looks once a frame at most (no IntersectionObserver, finding RM-3); canvas at most 2 device pixels per CSS pixel. Reduced motion: the finished figure at once, still, with every formula and mark, and no frame after it. No script: the band is his sentence alone (the strip shows only under html.js, its height the stylesheet's, so nothing shifts). Forced colours: the figure is hidden and a CanvasText rule marks the band's foot. Print: dark text on paper, no band, no figure.

Checked (29 Sep 2026, build/r15-masthead3/dist): frames every 250 ms at 1440 DPR 2, 1100 and 390; a browser sweep at 24 widths from 320 to 1920px and every 0.1 s of the story finds no ink inside any formula's box, no two formulas closer than 16px (26px from 375px up), nothing cut at the canvas's edges. tests/test_masthead.py holds the physics, the story's order and timing, the rows' clearances and the promises above.
