# rboxes: the four documents on My Research Areas, and the page's layout
Each card is an `<li class="rboxes__doc">`: a `--rule` hairline; his box label from slide 3 as an h3, verbatim as build.py transcribes it, whose link (`.rboxes__link`, to `doc/<slug>.html` or `presentation.html`) ends in the site's two-path arrow held to the last word by a no-break space, its `::after` spanning the card; and a foot, the Word line on the left (a link of its own above that span, never nested) and the 56px plate (stroke .75) on the right, the line's baseline on the plate's ground line. Card 4 says "177 slides, in the browser". A card without a link is `--wait`: no arrow, no wash, a `--muted` plate.
`render(docs_ready=False, sizes=None, hrefs=None)`. `docs_ready` turns the three Word lines into downloads, `download="<his file name>"`, the aria-label starting with the visible words; `sizes` `{slug: bytes}` prints bytes / 1e6 to two places, a slug left out keeps 0.11, 0.95 or 0.27 MB; `hrefs` `{slug: url}` points a line elsewhere (the Media Manager), else `doc/<slug>.docx`. Without `docs_ready` the lines read "Word file, coming soon". `_DOCS` keeps `slug`, `title`, `icon`, `mb` for tools/studio_build.py.
Lit, under a fine pointer: the warm `--wash` unfolds from the left (`--spring-draw`, back on `--spring-fast`), the arrow's shaft draws and its head slides 5px 60ms later, and the plate acts: its wavefronts step out 15% round their source, inner first, 50ms apart, or the record's trace is written again. No underline (under forced colours only). Over the Word line the card stands down (`:has()`). A key gets all of it at once, no travel, in a 2px `--focus` ring round the card; a press shows the wash at once and gives .98.
The relation: while a card is lit, `JS` marks the words of his text it takes further (`data-ref`, e.g. "sound source tracking") with a CSS Custom Highlight, `::highlight(rboxes-ref)`, in the same wash. His text is never touched; a phrase not found marks nothing. tests/test_rboxes.py checks each phrase against PROF_RESEARCH.
Arrival, once, in reading order: each hairline draws (`--spring-slow`), the label rises 8px 60ms behind, the foot 60ms later, then the plate's signal blooms from its source; cards 70ms apart (`--i`), done by 900ms. On screen at load it is CSS from the first frame; a card below the fold is held (`.is-out`) and arrives as it comes into view (`.is-in`), watched on scroll once a frame, no IntersectionObserver; focus shows it at once. The class is not `.rboxes__item`, so theme.py's generic reveal never stacks on it. Reduced motion: no arrival, no act, the wash fades in, the arrow stands whole.
It prints its own `<h2 id="rboxes-h">Research documents</h2>` (not "Documents", round-4 findings C3 and F8); page_research() must not add another. No `:root` writes, no em dashes, `JS` under 40 lines.
The page layout lives here too. page_research() writes `.rboxes-page > .rboxes-page__grid`: his text (`.col.col--his`), this section, `.col.rboxes-page__more` (PROF_RESEARCH_MORE: his heading "The educational sections (topics in the big picture menu)" and the paragraph he moved under it). Below 720px of page the three run in that order, the list two across from 480px of list and the h2 after it at 35px. From 720px (a 1100px window already, where his text says the documents are "on the right") the grid is `minmax(0,var(--measure)) minmax(280px,1fr)`, gap 40px, rows `auto 1fr`: his text and the section on the left, the cards stacked in a 280px column on the right spanning both rows, the h2 turned into a 12px sans caps label whose cap line is level with his first line's (computed from the fonts' metrics and `--lh`). The column ends at 771px, so all four cards are in the first screen of a 1280x800 window (and their plates at 1366x768).
