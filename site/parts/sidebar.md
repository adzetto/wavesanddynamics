# sidebar: the identity block and the desktop fold (parts/theme.py, build.py shell() and the base column rules)

Identity: the orange rule under the name is gone. The block reads as a letterhead in two groups: the name (serif 600, 20px) with "PhD, Assistant Professor" 5px under it (sans 400, --ink), then department and institute 10px lower (sans 400, --muted). The sans keeps one weight so nothing competes with the name. No small caps: neither Google font carries smcp.
Fold, above 1000px: a sidebar glyph beside the name hides the column, and the same glyph at the top left of the page brings it back. The column slides its width while .main's margin follows, 240ms on --ease; a second click midway reverses from where the motion is. Folded, .main keeps a 56px left margin so nothing scrolls under the show button. Hovering either button leans the glyph's pane edge the way the column will go.
State: a script in <head> sets <html data-side="open|closed"> from localStorage "wad:sidebar" before first paint, so a folded column stays folded from page to page with no slide on load, and a page restored from the back-forward cache takes the latest choice. Storage is read and written in try/catch; where it is refused the column opens, as on a first visit. Without JavaScript there is no data-side, no button, and the column is open.
Keyboard and screen readers: two real buttons, each with a title (tooltip and name), aria-controls="site-nav" and aria-expanded. A keyboard toggle moves focus to the button that stays on screen, and the folded column is visibility:hidden, so it leaves the tab order. Under reduced motion build.py's floor drops the transitions and the column goes or returns at once.
Short windows: on the desktop the identity row stays put and only the list scrolls, under a thin scrollbar in the column's blue, so a classic 15px scrollbar never wraps the name or moves the hide button. The 32px kept clear beside the name applies only where that button exists.
Double-click: the returning column puts the name, the link home, under the pointer, so shell()'s script drops a pointer press that lands off the buttons within 500ms of a fold. Keyboard clicks (detail 0) always go through.
Integrator: the fold uses (width > 1000px), the exact complement of the drawer's (max-width:1000px); build.py's #menu rule and parts/hero.py's .hero2__id rule use it too. Folded on the home page, theme.py shows the hero card's .hero2__id lines again. The phone drawer is unchanged.
Motion (27 Sep 2026, the client asked for fills under the pointer and a professional motion for his photo): a row's states are two layers under its words, a fill that wipes in from the column's edge (scaleX, mid spring) and a 3px rail grown from its middle (fast spring), light under the pointer and warm only on the current page; leaving fades them. In the Big Picture list the trunk lights from the row down to the pointed topic and runs on from topic to topic, the bead swells, the branch lights, the row fills from the trunk (parts/bpnav.py). A navy hairline draws round the photo from its top, both sides at once, on hover and focus; on the home page it stands warm. The first page of a visit (head_js() sets data-id-in, once a session, desktop column only) develops the photo out of a blur, settles the three lines 60ms apart and ends on the warm mark: the current row's rail, or the home page's ring. Keys take end states at once; reduced motion takes them with no travel; none of it loops.
