# Career (`parts/timeline.py`), round 10

- **Interface** `render(**_kw) -> str` returns `<section class="career">` with its h2 "Career" and an `<ol class="career__list">` of six roles, newest first. Each role is an h3 followed by its dates, place and note; the route beside them is `aria-hidden` (every fact it draws is in the words). build.py calls it through `part("timeline")` (or a candidate named by `WAD_TIMELINE`); parts/about.py places it between the biography and At a glance.
- **The route** A transit line: one lane for a university, a second for industry (Transtek, Renesas), an S-turn wherever the next role changes lane. Each stop is as long as the stay (12px plus 8px a year). The present post is a filled amber stop with a `--wash` halo; the stretch that brought him back to a university is amber, and past the present stop the line runs on and fades. The line is `--link` 45% into the paper, drawn in borders and SVG strokes, so it prints and follows forced colours.
- **Entrance (round 10)** Once, on the way down, one pen lays the route from the present post to the terminus, never ahead of the reader. On the first scroll, if the section was below the screen when the page opened, the script marks the list `.is-out` (only the route is hidden, never the words); a role is laid when it comes within 88% of the screen, every role above it first. Times come from the route (`--career-d`, `-f`, `-t`): the stop opens on `--spring-mid` from 60% as the line reaches it; 40ms on the pen passes through it for 55ms a year and the stop fills with `--link` light from its top (`clip-path`), so Austin's seven years fill seven times as long as Transtek's one; then the stretch, turn included, is wiped top to bottom at 0.6px/ms (`clip-path`, so a curve is never squashed) while the stop's light fades in 420ms. The present stop opens with one amber ring, dwells 180ms, and the pen leaves it on the amber stretch; the terminus bar settles in last. All fills are `backwards`, so a laid role is at rest. Print, preview, reduced motion, no IntersectionObserver: never hidden. A watchdog lays any role on screen the observer missed, once a second.
- **Hover** Mouse or trackpad only (`(hover:hover) and (pointer:fine)`): the stop turns amber, a `--wash` fill opens from the stop across the row (`scaleX` from the stop, 240ms; fades in 120ms), the role's own stretch draws in amber from the stop below and fills the stop (`clip-path`, 360ms), the rest of the route steps back to 45%, the dates turn amber, and one faint ring leaves the stop after 60ms. The ring now grows by transform (8px out on every side: `scale(2, (32 + 8y)/(16 + 8y))` for a stay of y years), not by `outline-offset` (round-4 finding RM-6). The present stop keeps its entrance ring in its animation list under the pointer, so leaving it does not ring again. The amber stretch keeps `clip-path`, which RM-6 allows: it draws a curve and fills a pill without squashing either.
- **Content** His words, unchanged; the role labels are ours in the case his biography gives them, the two "for ..." notes are his deck's. The bachelor's note reads "B.S. 2013", as his CV and cv.html do (round-4 finding C4). Ranges use a closed-up en dash; the present post reads "Since 2026".
- **Access** Nothing here is a link or a tab stop. Reduced motion: no entrance and no ring; hover states change in place. Forced colours: the route is `CanvasText`, the lit stretch and the present stop `Highlight`, the row fill and the stops' light go.
- **Scope** Selectors under `.career`, keyframes `career-`, tokens read only; `PAD`, `LANE` and `RAIL` (px) are shared by the CSS and the SVG turns. JS: the entrance only (30 lines; every height is read before anything is written, so laying forces no layout); without it the route is simply there. Tests: `tests/test_about.py`.
