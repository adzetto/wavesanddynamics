# Explore the topics
`render(items=None)`: six cards, rows 5 to 10 of his column, from build.py's `topic_items()`: `href`, `title`, `sub` (his sub-line, may be empty), `icon` (a key of `ICONS`: vibrations-waves, signal-processing, machine-learning, probability-statistics, python-programming, communication; an unknown key is guessed from the href and title), `soon`. Titles and sub-lines go in as given. `items=None` draws `DEFAULT_ITEMS`, the same six. The root keeps `id="topics"`; the script does nothing on a page without `.topics`.
Each card is a `<li>`: a 1px `--rule` hairline with the paper showing through; a head, `.topics__head`, holding the 28px subject icon (stroke 1.2, `--ink`, one `--accent` detail) and an `<h3>` link whose `::after` makes the whole card the target; his sub-line (serif 17px, `--body`); and for a page in preparation a quiet "In preparation" line (sans 12px caps, `--muted`) that is also the link's `aria-describedby`. Below 840px of list the cards are rows: the head lies across the card's two columns (a subgrid), the icon beside the title's first line, and the sub-line and the tag follow in the text column. From 840px, two rows of three, each card a subgrid of three of the list's rows: head, sub-line, tag. The heads sit down on the sub-lines, so in a row of three the titles end on one baseline, the sub-lines start on one line and the three "In preparation" tags share one; each icon stands 16px over its own title (over the two-line "Signal Processing, System Identification" it sits a line higher). The icon used to keep a line of its own at the top and floated 43px over a one-line title (round 4 finding F4). The second row stands 12px lower.
New icons in the family: Probability, Statistics is a histogram on its baseline with the fitted bell over it (the bell is the accent); Python / Programming is Python's prompt twice, a line already run and the waiting prompt with its cursor (the accent); Communication is a speech bubble carrying a rising result (the accent).
Hover (inside `(hover:hover)`): the card takes a 4% ink fill in 120ms (`--ease-state`), as every card and row on the site does (DESIGN_BRIEF 4; the round 4 radial bloom from the pointer is gone, findings RM-8 and RM-6), the title takes its underline, and the icon acts, the card's one motion: the wave runs in and its reflection out; the window sweeps the trace; the boundary draws; the bars rise 50ms apart and the bell is traced over them; a line is typed at the prompt in three keystrokes (steps(3), 300ms) as the cursor steps ahead, and taken back in 150ms; the chart draws in the bubble. All transitions, reversible midway, done by 400ms. Press: 7% fill. Keyboard focus: the fill, the underline and the icon's finished frame at once, with no fade and no travel, plus a 2px `--focus` ring round the card (finding RM-7).
Reduced motion: no reveal, no icon act; the fill simply appears. Reveal: armed only by a scroll that finds the first card within a quarter screen below the fold; the hairline draws, then the head, the sub-line and the tag rise 8px into place; rows of three cascade 70ms apart; the 1200ms failsafe stands down once a real intersection has revealed a card.
