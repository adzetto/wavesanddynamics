Probability makes sampling risk visible

A lot has 25 items and exactly one defect. Inspect 3 distinct items selected uniformly at random.

The model is specified

We know the lot size, defect countand sampling rule.

The probability is calculable

P(find the defect) = 3/25 = 12%

P(miss the defect) = 88%

A sample can miss a defect even when the sampling procedure is correct.
