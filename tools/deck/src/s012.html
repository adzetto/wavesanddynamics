Fitting and checking form a loop

Fit and diagnose iteratively. With a defensible existing model, new fitting may be unnecessary.

Fit

Estimate a distribution or regressionusing the observed sample.

Check

Compare model implications with data.Look at tails, residuals and dependence.

Methods: Q–Q plots, χ², K–S, Anderson–Darling, Shapiro–Wilk, residual checks.

Check data quality and model assumptions. A goodness-of-fit test cannot prove that a model is true.
