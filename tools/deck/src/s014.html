Frequentist and Bayesian: what are we asking?

Suppose we tested 25 concrete cylinders. We want to learn the unknown population mean strength μ.

FREQUENTIST: evaluate a procedure

“If we repeated this sampling process,how often would our method succeed?”

Imagine many possible samples of 25 tests.Each gives a different mean and interval.Assess the method across those samples.

The population mean stays fixed.The data and our estimates vary.

BAYESIAN: update uncertainty

“Given these measurements, whichvalues of μ are now more plausible?”

Start with a prior over plausible μ values.Use the data likelihood to reweight them.The posterior describes updated uncertainty.

μ can be physically fixed in both frameworks.The Bayesian distribution represents whatwe do not yet know about it.

Both use a data model. They differ in how they describe uncertainty about the unknown.
