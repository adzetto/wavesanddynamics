Frequentist and Bayesian inference: two frameworks

D = observed data; θ = unknown parameter. Likelihood p(D|θ) measures how well θ explains the observed data.

FREQUENTIST

θ is fixed and unknown. Samples vary.Build rules that work over repeated samples.

95% confidence: the procedure covers thefixed true value in 95% of repeated samples.

BAYESIAN

A distribution describes uncertainty about θ.Update it after observing the data D.

p(θ|D) ∝ p(D|θ) p(θ)

posterior ∝ likelihood × prior

The distinction concerns what probability describes and how we assess uncertainty.
