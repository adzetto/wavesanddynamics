Two kinds of spread: one test vs. the average of 25

Single cylinders scatter because the material varies. The average of 25 cylinders scatters much less, because high and low results cancel out.

One cylinder: material variability

Lands anywhere around μ; SD σ = 3 MPa.
More testing never shrinks σ.

More data

Average of 25: estimation uncertainty

Averages cluster near μ; SE = 0.6 MPa.
More tests shrink SE = σ/√n.

Var(X̄) = (1/n²) × nσ² = σ²/n

SE(X̄) = σ/√n = 3/√25 = 0.6 MPa

More tests sharpen our estimate of μ; they never make individual cylinders more alike.
