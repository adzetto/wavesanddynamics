Frequentist: why 1.96 in a 95% confidence interval?

Concrete strength: 25 independent normal tests, known population SD σ = 3 MPa, observed mean x̄ = 32 MPa.

1 Standardize the sample mean

μ = unknown population mean strength

SE = σ/√n = 3/√25 = 0.6 MPa

Z = (X̄ − μ)/SE ∼ N(0, 1)

2 Keep the central 95%

P(−1.96 ≤ Z ≤ 1.96) = 0.95

Each tail contains 2.5%.

1.96 is the 97.5th percentile of N(0, 1).

Here N(mean, variance) denotes a normal distribution.

3 Return to MPa

x̄ ± 1.96 SE = 32 ± 1.176

95% CI = [30.82, 33.18] MPa

1.96 comes from the standard normal distribution and the chosen two-sided 95% coverage.
