Similar intervals, different meanings

Same example: 25 tests, sample mean 32 MPa, known population SD 3 MPa. Bayesian prior: mean 30 MPa, SD 2 MPa.

QuestionFrequentist confidence intervalBayesian credible interval
Numerical answer[30.82, 33.18] MPa[30.71, 32.96] MPa
What does 95% mean?Across repeated samples, 95% of intervals built this way contain the fixed true mean.Given these data, model and prior, 95% posterior probability lies within this interval.
Where is uncertainty represented?In the sampling distribution of the estimator and the behavior of the procedure.In the posterior distribution over the unknown mean.
Why are the numbers close?The data locate the mean fairly precisely.The data receive about 92% weight; the prior supplies about 8%.

Different target: one future cylinder

Individual strengths still have SD 3 MPa. A prediction interval must include that variability.

Neither interval describes the range containing 95% of individual cylinder strengths.
