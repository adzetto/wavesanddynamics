Why regression is a probabilistic model

Regression does not just draw a line. It says that at each x, the response has a distribution, centered on the line.

The model

Y | x ~ N(β₀ + β₁x, σ²)

The line is the mean at each x; σ is the scatter.

What that gives us

Least squares = maximum likelihood here

Prediction intervals for a new test (slide 24)

Probabilities: P(Y < 25 MPa | x = 0.55) = 2.3%

Same idea, other outcomes

Counts: Poisson regression.Yes/no: logistic regression, fragility curves.

Illustrative data: β₀ = 75 MPa, β₁ = −80 MPa, σ = 3 MPa

The line is the mean of a distribution; the scatter around it is part of the model.
