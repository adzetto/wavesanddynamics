Uncertainty propagation: what goes in, what comes out

A deterministic equation gives one output per input combination. Uncertain inputs create a distribution of outputs.

Xᵢ = uncertain input i μᵢ = E[Xᵢ], its mean σᵢ = its standard deviation

Y = f(X₁, …, Xₙ): uncertain output f: the known calculation or physical model

A simple example with a simple equation: spring displacement Y = F/k

X₁ = force F: mean 100 N, SD 10 N X₂ = stiffness k: mean 20 N/mm, SD: 2 N/mmAssume normal F, positive lognormal k, and independence. At the mean inputs, Y = 5 mm.

We know how to calculate Y. Propagation tells us how uncertain Y is; the method depends on whether f is linear (next slide).
