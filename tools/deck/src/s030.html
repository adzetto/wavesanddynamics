Nonlinear f: why the tangent misses the mean

First order plugs in the mean stiffness and gets Y = 5.00 mm. A three-point check, with no calculus, shows why the true mean is a little higher.

F = 100 N, three stiffness values

k = 18 N/mm (softer): Y = 5.56 mm, +0.56

k = 20 N/mm (mean): Y = 5.00 mm

k = 22 N/mm (stiffer): Y = 4.55 mm, −0.45

Average of the 18 and 22 cases: 5.05 mm

Softer springs stretch extra.

Compare: Y is a straight line in F

F = 90 or 110 N gives Y = 4.5 or 5.5 mm

Average 5.00 mm: no shift

The tangent (orange) cannot see the bend,so first order misses this 0.05 mm.

Next: second order adds the curvature

½ × (∂²Y/∂k²) × σₖ² = ½ × 0.025 × 2²

= 0.05 mm, so μʏ ≈ 5.05 mm
