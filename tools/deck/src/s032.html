Nonlinear f: spring example, first vs. second order

Y is a straight line in F but a curve in k. The curve is what shifts the mean.

First order (tangent line at the means)

cF = ∂Y/∂F = 1/20 = 0.05 mm/N

ck = ∂Y/∂k = −100/20² = −0.25 mm2/N

μʏ ≈ 5.00 mm

σʏ² ≈ (0.05 × 10)² + (−0.25 × 2)²

σʏ ≈ 0.707 mm

Second order (tangent plus curvature)

∂²f/∂k² = 2F/k³ = 0.025 at the means

μʏ ≈ 5 + ½(0.025)(2²) = 5.05 mm

Only the stiffness-curvature term survives here:fFF = 0 and Cov(F, k) = 0.

Exact model moments for these inputs:Mean = 5.050 mm; SD = 0.716 mmMonte Carlo (slides 33–34) agrees.

Curvature shifts the mean displacement from 5.00 mm to 5.05 mm.
