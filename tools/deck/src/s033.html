Nonlinear f, method 3: Monte Carlo simulation

Evaluate the original model many times with random inputs; the outputs approximate the distribution of Y.

  • Works for linear and nonlinear models; no derivatives needed.
  • Most useful when the model is complex, curvature is strong, or tail probabilities matter.
  • Here it confirms the second-order mean of about 5.05 mm.

Four repeatable steps

1 Draw F and k from their input distributions.

2 Compute y = F/k for that pair.

3 Repeat N times to obtain y₁, …, yɴ.

4 Plot a histogram; calculate mean, SD,quantiles or a threshold probability.

Illustrative draws (F, k) and outputs:(100 N, 20 N/mm) gives 5.00 mm(110 N, 18 N/mm) gives 6.11 mm(90 N, 22 N/mm) gives 4.09 mm

200,000 simulations: mean ≈ 5.052 mmOutput SD ≈ 0.717 mm

Monte Carlo uses the original model; input assumptions and simulation error still matter.
