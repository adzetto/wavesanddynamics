Sample the inputs, run the original model for every sample, then read everything from the collected outputs, including the tail.

1 Draw input pairs (F, k)

2 Compute y for each pair, collect

3 More samples, smaller error

Tails: first order assumes a normal Y and gives P(Y > 7 mm) ≈ 0.23%. Simulation gives 0.74%, about 3 times more.

Accuracy: the error of the simulated mean shrinks like SD/√N. With N = 200,000 it is about 0.0016 mm.

Monte Carlo needs no derivatives and captures skew and tails; its price is many model runs.
