Choosing a propagation method: linear or nonlinear?

First ask: is f linear? Linear models have exact moments. Nonlinear models need a tangent, a curvature correction, or a simulation.

MethodWhat it doesUseful whenWhat to check
Exact linear momentsFor Y = b + ΣaᵢXᵢ, calculate mean and variance exactly.The model is linear over the input range.Moments alone do not determine every output distribution.
First-order TaylorReplace a curved response by its local tangent.Uncertainty is narrow; curvature is small.Compare the function with its tangent over plausible inputs.
Second-order TaylorKeep quadratic terms to represent curvature.Smooth, moderate curvature affects mean or spread.It is still local; neglected higher terms may matter.
Monte CarloSample inputs; evaluate the original model many times.Complex response, skewed outputs or quantiles matter.Dependence, convergence, computing cost and rare events.

Monte Carlo is useful for nonlinear propagation, but nonlinearity does not automatically require simulation.
