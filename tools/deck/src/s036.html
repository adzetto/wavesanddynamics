Capacity, demand and the safety margin

R = resistance or capacity. S = applied load effect or demand. G = R − S is the remaining safety margin.

Model: R ∼ N(100, 10²) kN and S ∼ N(70, 8²) kN; assume independence for this first case.Example realization: R = 95 kN, S = 80 kN gives G = 15 kN, a positive margin.

Derivation: ΔR = R − μR and ΔS = S − μS

Mean: E[R − S] = μR − μS = 30 kN

Variance: E[(ΔR − ΔS)²] = σR2 + σS2 − 2Cov(R, S) = 164 kN²

Failure is the joint event S > R, equivalently G < 0. Marginal curve overlap is not the failure probability.
