Why engineers care about representative records

The practical question is whether averaging the available history estimates the population quantity we want.

Operational modal analysis

FDD and SSI use estimated spectra or covariances from ambient vibration records.

Statistical mechanics

Compare long-time averages of system observables with the relevant ensemble average.

Wind and wave spectra

A record should represent the wind regime or sea state being modeled.

Finance / compounding

Average growth along one wealth path can differ from average wealth across paths.

Noise interferometry

Long cross-correlations can reveal propagation information under suitable source conditions.

Simulation checks

Compare many simulated realizations with target statistics. One time average is only one check.

Longer data help only if the recording explores the relevant behavior.
