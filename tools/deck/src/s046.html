Autocorrelation: what persists after a time shift?

Compare a signal with a delayed version of itself. The lag tells us how far apart the compared values are.

What to look for

Repeating peaks suggest periodicity.A slow envelope suggests persistence.Alternating signs suggest oscillation.

Mean-square versus variance

Autocorrelation R(0) = σ² + μ²Autocovariance C(0) = σ²They coincide when the mean is zero.

Correlation describes linear dependence. Zero correlation does not generally mean independence.
