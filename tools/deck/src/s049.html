Estimating a spectrum (2): Welch's method

Cut the record into overlapping segments, taper each with a window, compute a periodogram for each, and average. Averaging removes most of the jaggedness.

Welch average

P_W(f) = (1/K) · Σ P_k(f), K windowed segments

Here

L = 256 samples = 12.8 s, 50% overlap, K = 14. Frequency step 1/12.8 s ≈ 0.08 Hz.

More segments: smoother spectrum. Longer segments: finer frequency detail. Welch balances the two.
