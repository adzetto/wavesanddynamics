Poisson processes (2): two questions the model answers

The single number λ = 2 per hour answers both: how many trucks arrive in a time window, and how long we wait for the next one.

1 How many in 2 hours? Expected λt = 4

P(N = k) = e^(−λt)(λt)^k / k!, λt = 4. P(0) = 1.8%, P(4) = 19.5%, P(≥ 8) = 5.1%. Bars add up to 1.

2 How long until the next one?

f(w) = λe^(−λw), mean 1/λ = 0.5 h. P(wait > 1 h) = e^(−2) = 13.5% (shaded area).

Counts are Poisson, gaps are exponential: two faces of the same process.
