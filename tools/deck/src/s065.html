Kalman filtering: prediction meets a new measurement

A bridge displacement is estimated every hour. The sensor is noisy (SD 3 mm). The filter combines a simple model of how the displacement evolves with each new reading.

1 Predict

The model moves the estimate one step ahead: x̂⁻ = F x̂. Uncertainty grows.

2 Observe

A noisy reading arrives: z = H x + v.

3 Update

Move toward the reading by the gain K; uncertainty shrinks. Repeat every hour.

Result over 40 hours

Typical error: sensor alone 3.1 mm, Kalman estimate 1.6 mm.

Predict, observe, update, repeat: the estimate tracks the truth better than the raw readings.
