The Kalman loop: predict, observe, weigh, update

Every hour the filter goes once around this loop: the model proposes, the sensor reports, the gain decides.

1 Predict

Uncertainty grows.

x̂ₖ⁻ = F x̂ₖ₋₁

Pₖ⁻ = F Pₖ₋₁ Fᵀ + Q

2 Observe

A noisy reading arrives.

zₖ = H xₖ + vₖ

noise vₖ ∼ N(0, R)

3 Weigh

How much to trust the reading.

Kₖ = Pₖ⁻ Hᵀ (H Pₖ⁻ Hᵀ + R)⁻¹

K = P⁻/(P⁻ + R) for H = 1

4 Update

Uncertainty shrinks.

x̂ₖ = x̂ₖ⁻ + Kₖ (zₖ − H x̂ₖ⁻)

Pₖ = (I − Kₖ H) Pₖ⁻

next hourk → k + 1

The gain weighs the two sources: K near 0 keeps the prediction, K near 1 follows the reading.
