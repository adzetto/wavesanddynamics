One idea behind estimation: weight by precision

Two different examples from earlier in this guide. They look unrelated, but both combine an old estimate with new data using exactly the same rule.

Case A: concrete strength (slides 18–19)

Old 30 MPa (variance 4), data 32 MPa (variance 0.36). w = 4 / 4.36 = 0.92 → 31.83 MPa

Case B: bridge displacement (slides 65–66)

Old 10 mm (variance 4), data 14 mm (variance 9). w = 4 / 13 = 0.31 → 11.23 mm

Same rule: new = old + w × (data − old), w = var(old) / [var(old) + var(data)]

Different problems, same rule: each source counts in proportion to its precision.
