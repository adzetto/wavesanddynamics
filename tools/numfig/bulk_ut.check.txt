PROCESSING
  FMC: h_ij = recorded - half-space run at the same element distance |i - j| (same grid,
  so the direct waves cancel exactly): removes the initial pulse and the surface waves
  along the array, keeps every echo (flaw, back wall, side walls).
  TFM: I(x, z) = | sum_i sum_j H[h_ij](t0 + (|x - e_i| + |x - e_j|)/cL) |, H the analytic
  signal, t0 = 0.3 us, 0.1 mm pixels over x = +-14 mm, z = 0 ... 27 mm.
  (d) builds up the mean over transmitters 1 ... k (the partial sum over k), each exact,
  in dB of the complete image's peak, 30 dB range (the partial images peak at most +3.7 dB).
  The fan in (c) is the delay-law path of the flaw's
  peak pixel; each line's opacity is that pair's |H[h_ij]| at its delay (the pair's
  contribution to the pixel).
