MODEL
  Lamb waves in a free steel plate (plane strain), thickness d = 10 mm;
  E = 210 GPa, nu = 0.29, rho = 7850 kg/m3 (as Figures 4 and 5).
  c_L = 5920.9 m/s, c_T = 3220.1 m/s, c_plate = sqrt(E/(rho(1-nu^2))) = 5404.4 m/s,
  c_R = 2981.28 m/s (root of the Rayleigh equation).
  Rayleigh-Lamb equations, symmetric and antisymmetric, written real and pole free for
  real or imaginary p, q. Roots in phase velocity: a 6000 point log scan from 40 m/s to
  60 km/s, each sign change refined by Brent (1e-12 m/s). Branches S0, A0, A1 followed by
  continuation on a 0.5 kHz grid from 0.5 to 280 kHz, and checked against a full rescan
  at 10, 100, 200 and 275 kHz. Group velocity: central differences of exact roots, df = 2 Hz.
  Scan twice as fine: largest change of a root 7.1e-14 (relative).

OPERATING POINTS (f = 50 kHz, f d = 0.5 MHz mm)
  S0: c_p = 5391.3 m/s, c_g = 5364.2 m/s, dc_p/df = -0.544 (m/s)/kHz  (flat, non dispersive)
  A0: c_p = 1892.0 m/s, c_g = 2954.6 m/s, dc_p/df = +13.609 (m/s)/kHz  (steep, dispersive)

SYNTHESIS
  Burst: 3 cycle Hann window at 50 kHz, sampled every 0.05 us on one period of
  2.5 ms (line spacing 400 Hz); lines above 0.001 of the largest kept:
  353 lines, 0.40 to 158.80 kHz, each with its own k(omega) on S0 or A0.
  Error against a 10 ms period and lines down to 1e-6 (x = 0 to 3 m, t = -20 to 1400 us),
  as a share of the burst amplitude: S0 8.0e-04, A0 1.1e-03.
  The page sums the same lines (float32) at 861 points (2.5 mm) along the plate
  for every frame; against numpy at three moments: largest difference 4.5e-06.
  Echo widths (-6 dB of the envelope): burst 30.0 us, S0 echo 29.9 us,
  A0 echo 113.5 us (3.8 times the burst).
  Burst band (-6 dB of the spectrum): 33.6 to 66.4 kHz.
  Defect at D = 1.5 m: reflection 0.5, transmission sqrt(1 - R^2) = 0.866, frequency
  independent, no mode conversion; the transducer end does not reflect (the record stops
  once the echo is back).
