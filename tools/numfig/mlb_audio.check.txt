  mesh tone 30 f_r and harmonic 60 f_r; faulty: one click per output turn (ratio 1:3),
  2200 Hz, decay 0.3 ms; white noise 0.02. STFT: Hann 128 samples (16 ms), hop 32 (4 ms); 65 bins x 247 frames;
  dB over 50 dB, 8 bit. Filters 9 wide: horizontal, rising (slope 0.6 bin/frame), vertical; ReLU;
  global average; dense 3-4-2 (ReLU, softmax), gradient descent 3000 epochs, rate 0.5.
