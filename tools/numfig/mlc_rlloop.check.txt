CHECKS
  probabilities sum to 1 in every row: max |sum - 1| = 1.1e-16
  the rewarding step: Q(up at (4, 3)) 0.000 -> 0.500 = 0 + 0.5 (1 - 0);
  pi(up | (4, 3)) 0.2917 -> 0.6250 = eps/4 + (1 - eps) = 0.6250: the agent becomes more likely to repeat the move that reached the goal.
  every other step pays -0.02, so the action just tried drops to eps/4 = 0.125 while untried actions (Q = 0) share the rest.
  table after the episode equals the run's own snapshot after episode 1: max |dQ| = 0.0e+00
