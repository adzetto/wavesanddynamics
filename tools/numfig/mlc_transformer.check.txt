nf-mlc-transformer: Figure 24, a transformer filling in 'My [MASK] barks at night'

MODEL (mlc_text.tiny_transformer): token embedding (8) + sinusoidal position,
  2 blocks of [2 head scaled dot-product attention -> add and norm -> feed forward
  (8 -> 16 -> 8, ReLU) -> add and norm], a linear read out at the masked position.
  Attention both ways: every token attends to every token of its sentence (only the
  padding of shorter sentences is masked before the softmax), as in BERT.
  Trained as BERT is to fill in a masked word: each of the 88 sentences once per word, that
  word replaced by [MASK] (530 examples, 169 words), cross entropy, Adam 0.01,
  6000 full batch steps, torch seed 0 (torch 2.14.1+cpu).
  final loss 0.0415, the corpus's own minimum 0.0414 (each masked sentence's own
  word frequencies); masked word right 0.977 of the time (the corpus is ambiguous in
  places: 'The [MASK] ate its food' is cat once and dog once).

CHECK 1: the predicted word against the corpus's own counts
  corpus for 'My [MASK] barks at night': dog 1

  the word after the gap decides it: 'My [MASK] purrs at night' (corpus: cat 1)
    gives cat 1.000, dog 0.000
  where the corpus is split, so is the model: 'The [MASK] ate its food' (corpus: cat 1, dog 1)
    gives cat 0.504, dog 0.496
  a sentence not in the corpus: 'The dog [MASK] on the mat' gives sat 1.000, its 0.000
  [MASK] weighs the words on both sides of it: in block 2 head 1 it puts
