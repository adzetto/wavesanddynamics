nf-periodic: Figure 6, propagation zones of a beam on periodic pin supports

MODEL
  Euler-Bernoulli beam, infinite, pin supports every L; harmonic e^{i w t}.
  Exact span transfer matrix on (rotation, moment) between two pins, from the
  closed form field matrix (Krylov functions); propagation constant mu per span
  from cosh mu = T11 = S - T U / V at z = k L (Mead 1970, monocoupled periodic).
  steel bar 40 x 10 mm, E = 210 GPa, rho = 7850 kg/m^3: EI = 700.0 N m^2, m = 3.140 kg/m, L = 0.5 m (L/h = 50, EB valid)
  No mesh: every quantity is closed form; roots by Brent's method to 1e-15 in z.
