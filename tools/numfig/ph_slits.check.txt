MODEL (lengths in wavelengths, lambda = 1, k = 2 pi)
  a line source (a point in 2D) at x = -100, on the axis; an opaque screen at x = 0 with two slits
  of width a = 1.2, centres y = +2.4 (A) and -2.4 (B), so d = 4.8; the detecting screen at
  x = L = 12, drawn over |y| < 11. ad / (lambda L) = 0.48: the far-field side of
  the document's ad / (lambda L) > 1 for two separate bands.
  incident field U_inc = H0(k r); each slit's field behind the screen is the first Rayleigh-Sommerfeld
  (Huygens-Fresnel) integral in 2D over its aperture, with U_inc in the slit and 0 on the screen:
    U_s(x, y) = (i k / 2) Int_slit U_inc(0, y') H1(k rho) x / rho dy',  rho = |(x, y) - (0, y')|
  time harmonic, the field Re{U e^{-i w t}}. (a) one polarization: U = U_A + U_B, intensity |U_A + U_B|^2.
  (b) perpendicular polarizations: E = U_A e_A + U_B e_B with e_A . e_B = 0, so |E|^2 = |U_A|^2 + |U_B|^2.
  all fields divided by |U_inc| at the slits (0.031831). B is A mirrored in the axis (the source is on it).
  quadrature: 800 Gauss-Legendre points per slit; screen sampled every 0.01.

THE FIELD ON THE PAGE
  A's field on a grid every 1/6 wavelength, 73 x 133 points, stored as its envelope
  E = U e^{-i k rho} sqrt(rho + 0.5) (rho from A's centre), int16 (rounding 1.6e-05 of max |E|); the page
  rebuilds U at its own pixel size by Catmull-Rom and the exact carrier e^{i k rho}; B is A mirrored.
  rebuilt against the integral at 3000 random points, in units of max |E|:
    beyond 0 wavelength of the slit's centre: largest error 4.2e-02
    beyond 0.5 wavelength of the slit's centre: largest error 1.5e-02
    beyond 1 wavelength of the slit's centre: largest error 2.7e-03
    beyond 2 wavelength of the slit's centre: largest error 7.9e-04
  crest lines: where Re{U e^{-i w t}} peaks, darkness g ((cos(phi - w t) - 0.35) / 0.65)^3, phi = arg U;
  g = min(1, |U| sqrt(rho + 0.5) / n0) takes out the 2D spreading 1 / sqrt(rho) so the far lobes stay
  visible (rho from each slit in (b), from the midpoint in (a)); n0 = 1.4388: A alone peaks at 0.85
  on the screen, so where A and B add in (a) the lines are darkest and where they cancel they vanish.
  colour is polarization: (a) one field, blue; (b) A blue, B crimson; the source's field grey.
  crest lines rather than the engine's DIVERGING map: its crimson arm would mean a negative field in (a)
  and slit B's light in (b); as crest lines one colour means one polarization in both panels.
  the opaque screen is drawn 0.34 wavelength thick, centred on the aperture plane; the field within 0.17
  of the plane lies under it, where the grid's error is largest. Just behind it, in units of max |E|:
    0.17 <= x < 0.35: largest error 5.0e-02
    0.35 <= x < 0.5: largest error 1.9e-02
    0.5 <= x < 1: largest error 8.9e-03
  the source's field H0(k r) is drawn near the source (r < 2.5, a 42 deg cone) and in front of the slits
  (the last 2.1 wavelengths, |y| < 3.3, 100 from the source); the break mark on the axis stands for the
  95 wavelengths between. The phase of H0 beyond k r - pi/4 is tabulated from scipy for r < 3.

THE PHOTONS
  10000 per screen; each lands at y drawn from the screen's intensity as a probability density (inverse
  CDF, linear between the fine grid's points) and anywhere across the drawn plate's width; arrival
  times a Poisson process with expected count n(tau) = n0 (e^{tau/tau0} - 1), tau0 = 0.7303 s,
  4 expected in the first second and 10000 by 6.5 s (time rescaling of unit exponential gaps);
  seeds 1927 (a) and 1961 (b).
    (a) Kolmogorov-Smirnov against the density: D = 0.0134, p = 0.06; histogram of 88 bins,
        chi^2 = 96.1 on 87 degrees of freedom, p = 0.24; 4 landed in the first second,
        the last at 6.506 s
    (b) Kolmogorov-Smirnov against the density: D = 0.0040, p = 1.00; histogram of 88 bins,
        chi^2 = 82.7 on 87 degrees of freedom, p = 0.61; 5 landed in the first second,
        the last at 6.486 s
  the histogram: bins of 0.25 wavelength, counts / (max(n, 800) bin), in the same units as the
  curve (the probability density), so its bars grow with the first photons and then settle onto the curve.
  both panels use one density scale: the same light, in (a) moved into fringes twice the smooth level.

TIME
  one optical period per second of the page (for 600 nm light, time slowed 5e+14 times); the crests
  move lambda per second. The intro draws the field along its own path: the source's waves (0.08 to 0.4 s),
  then the slits' fields out to the screen (0.34 to 0.82 s): a drawing, not the switch-on transient, which
  would take the light 12 periods (12 s here) to reach the screen. The photons start at 0.9 s; the cycle
  is 10 s: they land, the histogram holds, then fades in 0.35 s and starts again.
  poster (print, reduced motion) at t = 8.01 s: all 10000 photons landed in both panels.
