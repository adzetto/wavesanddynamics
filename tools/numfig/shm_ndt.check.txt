generator: tools/numfig/shm_ndt.py, model: tools/numfig/shm_fdtd.py

MODEL
  2D plane-strain elastodynamics, velocity-stress on a staggered grid (Virieux 1986),
  voids as cells of zero density and stiffness (EFIT, Fellinger et al. 1995); every face
  of the steel and of a crack is traction free. P, S and Rayleigh waves and mode
  conversion are all in it.
  steel: c_L = 5900 m/s, c_T = 3230 m/s, rho = 7850 kg/m3
  source: uniform normal traction on the probe or element face, 2 MHz, 3 cycles,
  Hann window (1.5 us); receiver: the mean normal velocity over the same face
  grid: dx = 0.1 mm (29.5 points per P wavelength, 16.1 per S), CFL 0.6
