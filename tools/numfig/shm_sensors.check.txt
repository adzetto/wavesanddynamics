  a flexural cantilever (walls, EI) and a shear cantilever (frames, GA) acting together,
  EI u'''' - GA u'' + m u.. = p(x, t), fixed at the ground (u = u' = 0), free at the roof

  finite elements: 40 Hermite cubics (u, u' at each node), consistent mass; GA enters as
  the integral of GA u' v' (the tension-string matrix)
