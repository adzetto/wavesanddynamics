MODEL
  A plane sound wave in air, c = 343 m/s (20 C), from the direction theta (measured
  from the x axis, on the side it comes from), s = (cos theta, sin theta). A sensor at p
  hears the front at t(p) = t0 - p.s / c. The front that has just reached the reference
  sensor n has the Additional Distance Dd = (p_n - p_m).s still to go before it reaches m;
  m hears it tau = Dd / c later.
  gamma, as he draws it: the angle at n between the chord to m and the line from n to the
  origin. The chord's direction is then beta_n + pi - gamma, and
    Dd = d_{n,m} cos(theta + gamma - beta_n).
  Symphony (Wang et al. 2020, eq. 6) writes the relative delay as
  d_{n,m} cos(pi - (gamma - beta_n) - theta) / v; cos(pi - x) = -cos x, so it is the same
  length, its sign set by which of the two sensors is counted first.
