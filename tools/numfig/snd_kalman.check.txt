  it is the first seed whose four innovations lie within 0.6 to 1.6 of their standard

DISPLAY
  one filter step (Dt = 0.5 s) every 4.5 s of the page: time slowed 9 times, said in the
  parameter line. The target flies at its constant 40 m/s all the while (4.44 m per second of
  the page) and passes the place it is measured at as the measurement arrives (n TS into the
  loop). Then: the measurement grows in (0.3 s); the update, 0.3 to 0.9 s, slides the gain's
  pointer from 0 to K_n while the estimate moves and narrows with it; the finished step (its three
  Gaussians, K_n and the three uncertainties' values) holds 2.75 s; the step's prediction
  and measurement leave (0.25 s) and the estimate moves on with the model and widens into the next
  prediction (0.6 s), arriving at the next measurement. The first estimate holds from the start
  until the first prediction (3.9 s); after the last step's hold the picture fades (0.5 s) and
  the first estimate fades in (0.4 s) with the target at its start: a loop of 22.5 s, the
  target's 2.5 s, and nothing rewinds. The poster is step 2, 1.2 s after its measurement.
  The shapes between two steps are a transition; where each step lands is the filter's numbers,
  and the K_n printed is always the step's own. His names sit under their Gaussians' means,
  pushed apart only as far as they must be; the uncertainties, his words, are listed with
  their values under them. The y axis is the Gaussians' value, a probability density in 1/m.
  Type: names 17 and their symbols 21, the legend's symbols and K_n 20, so the subscripts that
  tell n|n from n|n-1 are set at 14 to 14.7 (the engine's scripts are 0.7 of their label).
