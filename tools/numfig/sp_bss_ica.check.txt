MODEL (sp_bss_lib, shared with Figure 11)
  fs = 500 Hz, record T = 4 s, N = 2000 samples; drawn: the first 4 s, every second sample (1000).
  s1: (1 + 0.5 sin 2 pi 0.4 t)(sin 2 pi 3t + 0.45 sin(2 pi 6t + 0.7) + 0.25 sin(2 pi 9t + 1.3)).
  s2: impacts ringing an 11 Hz mode, zeta = 0.08, his seeded train (mulberry32(21)), here 8 impacts:
      (0.100 s, -0.724), (0.520 s, -0.738), (1.305 s, +0.673), (1.821 s, +0.594), (2.275 s, +0.670), (2.583 s, +0.603), (3.166 s, +0.526), (3.709 s, -0.775)
  both standardized; A = [[1, 0.7], [0.45, 1]]; noise: two standardized white Gaussian channels, his
  Box-Muller on mulberry32(7 + T) = mulberry32(11); sigma^2 = 1.2025 10^(-SNR/10) (the weaker sensor's SNR).
  In this record: corr(s1, s2) = -0.0830; excess kurtosis s1 -0.263 (sub-Gaussian), s2 +1.484.
  Method: centre; W from the sample covariance (W C W' = I, rows the principal axes over root
  eigenvalues); z = W x; y = R(theta) z, R = [[cos, sin], [-sin, cos]], theta in [-45, 45) deg maximizing
  K = |kurt y1| + |kurt y2|. K(theta) = |k(theta)| + |k(theta + 90 deg)|, k a quartic in (cos, sin) through the
  fourth moments m40, m31, m22, m13, m04 of z: maximized on a 0.01 deg grid, refined (bounded Brent, 1e-13 rad).
  Order and sign cannot be known by any blind method: outputs are paired with sources by the larger of
  |G11 G22| and |G12 G21|, G = R W A, and signs taken from G. SIR of a source: G_ij^2 / G_ik^2 in the output
  that carries it (unit-variance sources); rho: Pearson correlation of that output with the source.

DISPLAY
  Every stage is a 2 x 4 matrix on u = [s1, s2, n1, n2] (his device); the page draws M u: sources [I 0],
  sensors Aug = [A, sigma I], whitened W Aug, rotated R(theta) W Aug (theta turning from 0 to theta*),
  estimates. Between stages the matrices are interpolated (his device); the rotation is a true rotation.
  Each stage is drawn in units of its RMS: 1 for s, z and y; the sensors carry the factor printed at the
  axes (x 1.16 at 10 dB, x 34.67 at -30 dB). The arrows in (b) are the images of the
  two source axes, the first two columns of the stage's matrix (2.6 units long per unit); they fade out
  where their length falls under 0.55 (the signal sinks under the noise). (c) one scale for every SNR
  (0 to 2), so that noise visibly flattens K(theta). The cursor plays the 4 s in real time.
  The dashed source over each estimate is that source times the estimate's least-squares share of it.
  No 'new noise' control: his two pages draw one seeded record (mulberry32(7) and (7 + T)) and so does
  this figure; item 7 shows how other records scatter.
