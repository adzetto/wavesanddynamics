  Method (his): Welch auto and cross spectra (periodic Hann, 50% overlap, no scaling: S_ij = mean conj(X_i) X_j);
  floor f_i = his median of S_ii over bins 1..L/2 (element floor(n/2) of the sorted values); a bin is a band if
  (S_11 - f_1)/(f_1/sqrt K) + (S_22 - f_2)/(f_2/sqrt K) > 4 (at least the best 4); M = sum over the bands of
  [[S_11 - f_1, Re S_12], [Re S_12, S_22 - f_2]]; W0 M W0' = I (his PCA convention), then W = W0 scaled so that
  W x has unit RMS (the scale is free); D_k = W0 M_k W0'; the rotation theta minimizing J = sum (R D_k R')_12^2,
  in closed form (h_k = ((D_k)_12, ((D_k)_22 - (D_k)_11)/2), J = v' G v, v = (cos 2 theta, sin 2 theta), v the
  eigenvector of G = sum h_k h_k' of the smaller eigenvalue), taken in [0, 90) deg (a quarter turn is the
  permutation); y = R W x; Wiener gain per output max(0, P - o N)/P, N = B_i1^2 f_1 + B_i2^2 f_2, o = 1 + 2/sqrt(K),
  smoothed [1 2 1]/4 over bins, applied through a zero-padded FFT with linear interpolation (his applyGain).
  Order and sign matched as in Figure 10; SIR per source from G = R W A; rho: Pearson correlation.
