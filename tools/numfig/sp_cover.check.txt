The cover (input, system, output, identification): check of tools/numfig/sp_cover.py

MODEL
  System: the resonator of sp_model, h(t) = exp(-zeta w_n t) sin(w_d t), f_n = 1 Hz, zeta = 0.1.
  Input: a linear sweep 0.3 to 1.7 Hz over 12 s, cosine tapered over 1 s at each end,
  sampled every 0.01 s and linear between samples (float32, as the page gets it).
  Output: y = x * h, exact for that input (sp_model.foh_filter); measured with white Gaussian
  noise sigma = 0.02568, 20.0 dB below the output's power (seed 2026).
  Estimation: least squares, J(f_n, zeta) = mean (y_meas - yhat(f_n, zeta))^2, the maximum
  likelihood estimate for this Gaussian noise.
  Optimization: gradient descent from (0.85 Hz, 0.27), step 0.12, central differences
  (1e-7) for the gradient, stopped within 2e-5 of the least squares solution; the plane is drawn
  at one scale for f_n and zeta, so each step is perpendicular to the contours as drawn.
  18 steps. Contours of J at 1.6, 3, 6, 12, 25, 50, 100, 170 times its minimum, on an 81 x 76 grid.

VALIDATION
  1. The output against the equation of motion y'' + 2 zeta w_n y' + w_n^2 y = w_d x(t),
     integrated sample by sample (DOP853, rtol 1e-12): largest difference 1.7e-14
     (2.6e-14 of max |y|).
  2. Least squares solver (trust region, scipy): f_n = 0.999538 Hz, zeta = 0.100412;
     the descent ends 8.9e-06 from it. Gradient of J there: 1.7e-11 (J = 6.7191e-04,
     sigma^2 = 6.5940e-04). Central differences 1e-5 against 1e-7 on the path: 6.2e-09 (relative).
  3. The estimate's spread: Gauss-Newton covariance s^2 (J^T J)^-1 against 500 repeated
     experiments (new noise, seeds 10000 on, each solved by least squares):
     f_n: 4.04e-04 Hz against 3.85e-04 Hz (+5 %), zeta: 4.01e-04 against 3.74e-04 (+7 %);
     mean error of the 500 estimates (bias): f_n -2.3e-05 Hz, zeta -8.0e-06, that is
     -1.3 and -0.5 standard errors of the mean: no significant bias.
     The estimate shown, (0.9995 Hz, 0.1004), lies 1.1 and 1.0 standard deviations from the truth.
  4. The page computes the model's output itself (the same recursion in complex arithmetic):
     against numpy at the first guess and at the estimate, largest difference 7.2e-14.

DISPLAY
  One descent step every 0.16 s, each a Motion spring glide; the readout on the feedback line is
  the current (f_n, zeta). The 95 % confidence region (about +-0.001) is below one drawing unit
  at this scale and is not drawn.
