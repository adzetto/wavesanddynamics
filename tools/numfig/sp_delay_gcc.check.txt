MODEL (his page, 'Delayed signal recovery, method 1: GCC and Wiener filter')
  x1(t) = s(t) + n1(t), x2(t) = s(t - tau) + n2(t), tau = 48 ms = 24 samples at fs = 500 Hz,
  N = 2048 samples (4.096 s). n1, n2 white Gaussian, independent; SNR = signal power / noise power
  of one record, sigma = 10^(-SNR/20) on the standardized signal (mean 0, RMS 1 over the record).
  Signals (his): impacts = sum of 8 impacts (t0, a) = (0.15, 1), (0.6, -0.7), (1.05, 0.85), (1.45, 0.6), (2.05, -1), (2.6, 0.75), (3.05, -0.55), (3.55, 0.9),
  each a exp(-zeta w_n u) sin(w_d u), f_n = 11 Hz, zeta = 0.08, divided by 0.27; harmonic =
  (1 + 0.5 sin 2 pi 0.4 t)(sin 2 pi 3 t + 0.45 sin(2 pi 6 t + 0.7) + 0.25 sin(2 pi 9 t + 1.3)), the same
  formula as his blind source separation pages (their impact trains differ, so the delay pages' is kept);
  both = impacts + 0.6 harmonic. The delayed copy is the formula at t - tau, scaled as s: a true delay.
  Method (his, line for line): Welch spectra (periodic Hann, 256 samples, 50% overlap, K = 15 segments);
  GCC = IFFT[W conj(A) B] with 4,096-point zero padded FFTs of the two records and the weight
  W = g2 / (1 - g2), g2 = |S12|^2 / (S11 S22) clipped at 0.99, linear between Welch bins, read over
  lags -125 .. 125 samples; delay = the lag of its largest value (whole samples, 2 ms); x2 advanced by it
  (FFT phase ramp, circular), averaged with x1; Wiener gain G = max(0, Re S12' - 2 sqrt(S11' S22'/(2K)))
  / S_avg (at most 1) from the aligned records' spectra, applied to the average; the output standardized.
  Every choice is computed here: 3 signals x 11 SNRs (-30 to 20 dB, 5 dB steps), one noise
  draw scaled by sigma (seed 25: numpy default_rng, SeedSequence spawned per sensor; shot 1 of Figure 13).
