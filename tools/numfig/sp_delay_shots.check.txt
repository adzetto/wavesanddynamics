MODEL (his page, 'Delayed signal recovery, method 2: shot averaging, GCC and Wiener filter')
  As Figure 12 (sp_delay_gcc.check.txt): one signal, tau = 48 ms = 24 samples at fs = 500 Hz, N = 2048,
  his three signals, his method line for line. His two additions:
  - shots: the excitation fired M times, the signal repeated exactly, new white Gaussian noise in every
    shot, the records averaged. His page scales one noise draw by 1/sqrt(M); here the shots are drawn:
    each sensor's own generator (seed 25, numpy default_rng, SeedSequence spawned per sensor) gives
    shot after shot, 1024 of them, and the record of M shots is the average of the first M (nested:
    the 64-shot average holds the 16-shot one; M = 1 is Figure 12's record).
  - the delay refined below one sample: d = i + (y0 - y2) / (2 (y0 - 2 y1 + y2)), at most half a sample,
    through the GCC peak y1 at lag i and its neighbours; the record advanced by the fractional d.
  Every choice is computed here: 3 signals x 11 SNRs of one shot x 5 numbers of shots.
  The noise draw: seed 25, by the rule in sp_delay_gcc.check.txt (typical at both default settings).
