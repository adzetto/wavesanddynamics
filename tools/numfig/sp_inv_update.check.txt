  two-storey shear building, floor masses m = 100 t each; K = 10^6 [k1 + k2, -k2; -k2, k2] N/m,

  misfit Phi = ((f1 - f1m)/(0.003 f1m))^2 + ((f2 - f2m)/(0.003 f2m))^2 [+ ((r - rm)/(0.05 rm))^2 with the

  closed form: T = k1 + 2 k2, R = sqrt(k1^2 + 4 k2^2), mu2 = (T + R)/2, mu1 = 2 k1 k2/(T + R) (MN/m),
  lambda = 10^6 mu / m, f = sqrt(lambda)/(2 pi), r = (k1 + R)/(2 k2).

THE TWIN (Hadamard's second condition, uniqueness, fails)
  the frequencies fix the trace and determinant of M^-1 K: k1 + 2 k2 = L1 + L2 = 278.324200 and
  k1 k2 = L1 L2 = 9580.579432 (MN/m, L = (2 pi f)^2 m / 10^6: L1 = 40.240360, L2 = 238.083840), so
  2 k2^2 - (L1 + L2) k2 + L1 L2 = 0 has two roots: (k1, k2) = (124.8467, 76.7388) next to the
  true building and its twin (153.4775, 62.4233) MN/m. The twin of any (k1, k2) is (2 k2, k1/2):
  it keeps k1 + 2 k2 and k1 k2, |det| of the map is 1, and its fixed points are the fold line k1 = 2 k2,
  where the Jacobian of (k1 + 2 k2, k1 k2), k1 - 2 k2, vanishes.
  both reproduce the data: f1 = 3.192648158, 3.192648158 Hz; f2 = 7.765780140, 7.765780140 Hz (measured
  3.192648158, 7.765780140); their mode ratios differ: r = 2.1025 and 2.8140 (measured 2.0400).
  with exact data the pair would be (120, 80) and (160, 60) (r = 2 and 3). A 0.3% error in f1 and
