Figure 13, coverage of bulk and guided wave testing: check of tools/numfig/ut_coverage.py

MODEL (a): BULK WAVE PROBE
  Circular piston, D = 10 mm, centre frequency 5 MHz, in the steel of Figure 12
  (c_L = 5920.9 m/s): lambda = 1.184 mm, k a = 26.53.
  Field: the Rayleigh integral in edge wave form (direct plane wave under the face minus the
  rim's diffracted wave), a 4096 point sum round the rim.
  Pulsed beam: RMS over a Gaussian band, 60 % at -6 dB, 25 frequencies over +-3 sigma;
  grid 0.25 mm, 40 mm wide by the plate's 10 mm depth.
  Insonified zone: at each depth, where the pulse echo response (field squared) is within
  -6 dB of that depth's maximum; the zone's outer edge is drawn.

VALIDATION (a)
  1. Edge wave form against the Rayleigh surface integral itself (200 x 400 point
     Gauss-Legendre by trapezoid quadrature) at 7 points from z = 1 to 30 mm:
     largest relative difference 3.6e-14.
  2. On axis against 2|sin(k (sqrt(z^2 + a^2) - z)/2)|, z = 0.5 to 80 mm: largest difference 5.0e-14.
     Last on-axis maximum (near field length): 20.816 mm against (D^2 - lambda^2)/(4 lambda) =
     20.816 mm (+0.000 mm, the grid step is 0.004 mm);
     D^2 f/(4c) = 21.11 mm.
  3. Far field (R = 0.4 m, 19 near field lengths), -6 dB half angle: one way 4.790 deg against
     asin(2.2151/(k a)) = 4.789 deg (2 J1(u)/u = 1/2); pulse echo
     3.492 deg against asin(1.6163/(k a)) = 3.493 deg (0.514 lambda/D).
  4. Pulsed beam with 49 frequencies instead of 25: zone edge moves at most 0.00 um.

COVERAGE (a)
  Zone width over the depth: 6.41 to 9.29 mm (7.40 mm at mid depth).
  Scan index 5 mm, below the narrowest zone: 400 positions over the 2 m span,
  the first with the probe flush with the free end (centre at 5 mm), the last at 2 m.
  Union of all zones: the weakest pulse echo response anywhere in the plate is -4.90 dB
  (above -6 dB: the span is covered completely).
  Flaw 1 (lamination 24 mm long, at x = 0.6 m, 6 mm deep) lies in a zone
  at positions 117 to 123 only.
  Flaw 2 (lamination 24 mm long, at x = 1.4 m, 3.5 mm deep) lies in a zone
  at positions 277 to 283 only.
  The scan's pace on the page is designed (slow first steps, then fast, easing while a flaw
  is under the probe; 7.8 s in all); the positions and what each covers are the model's.

MODEL (b): GUIDED WAVE
  The S0 mode of Figure 12 (guided_ut.py): Rayleigh-Lamb roots, 3 cycle Hann burst at 50 kHz,
  353 spectral lines each with its own k(omega); c_p = 5391.3 m/s, c_g = 5364.2 m/s.
  Two flaws, each reflecting 0.25 and transmitting sqrt(1 - R^2) = 0.9682 (frequency
  independent, schematic), single scattering: the echo from flaw 2 passes flaw 1 twice.

VALIDATION (b)
  Echo 1: envelope peak (Hilbert) 253.8 us against burst centre + 2x/c_g = 253.7 us (+0.10 us);
     the flaw placed at c_g (t - t_c)/2 = 0.6003 m (true 0.6 m).
  Echo 2: envelope peak (Hilbert) 552.3 us against burst centre + 2x/c_g = 552.0 us (+0.32 us);
     the flaw placed at c_g (t - t_c)/2 = 1.4009 m (true 1.4 m).
  Coverage front: the burst centre, at c_g, reaches the next sensor position (2 m) at
  402.8 us.
  The page's own sum against numpy at three moments: largest difference 7.8e-06.
