  source at (0.7, 0.205) m; sensors at (0.085, 0.075), (0.915, 0.085), (0.885, 0.33), (0.125, 0.32) m
  arrival times: t1 = 116.31 us, t2 = 45.56 us, t3 = 41.31 us, t4 = 108.50 us

  f =   10 kHz (fd = 0.10 MHz mm): c_S0 = 5403.93 m/s, c_plate = 5404.44 m/s, -9.4e-05
